Zero — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026

The short version: Zero doesn't track you. No analytics, no advertising, no data sales — and Zero never learns from you or its viewers. Your stuff lives in your browser first; the little our servers must hold is listed below, with automatic deletion.

1. What stays on your device

Zero is local-first. Your notes, tasks, settings, SchoolAhead school data, ZeroTube watch history and subscriptions, and your Zero Computer pairing key are stored in your own browser (localStorage / extension storage). They never leave your device unless you ask for something that needs a server — like asking the AI a question. Clear your browser's site data any time and it's gone.

2. What Zero's servers keep

Free hosting means tiny, capped storage. These are the only things Zero's servers write, each with automatic deletion:

That's the complete list. No profiles, no history logs, no backups of your content.

3. The Zero Computer extension

  • Page content: while a task you started is running, the extension reads the page's title, text, links, and form fields — plus screenshots so you can watch progress.
  • Pairing key: a random 32-character key stored only in your browser. It authenticates your own task requests; nobody else can drive your browser with it.
  • It never reads, fills, or submits password fields; never logs in as you; never buys or pays for anything.
  • It doesn't run when you're not using it — only while a task you started is in progress.

4. What Zero never does

  • No analytics, cookies-for-tracking, or fingerprinting. Zero itself sets no tracking cookies.
  • No advertising and no selling or sharing data with third parties.
  • No learning from you or your viewers. The feature that let Zero learn from usage was removed on October 19, 2026 at the owner's direction, and it isn't coming back.
  • AI providers (Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter, Mistral, Pollinations) receive only the text of the question you ask — never your notes, tasks, or identity.
