ⓘ What Zero can and can't open here
  • Works here: most articles, docs, wikis, stores, and search results open right inside Zero.
  • Opens in a new tab instead: sites that block embedding (Google, YouTube, many banks and logins — they send an X-Frame-Options header). Zero detects this and hands you a real tab.
  • Won't play here: DRM-protected video (Netflix, Hulu, Disney+) needs the real app or browser — no embedded viewer can play it.
  • Broken-looking page? Use Reader view or Open in new tab — you'll always get a way through, never a dead page.

Recents stay in this browser only — nothing leaves your device.
