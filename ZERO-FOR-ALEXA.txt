WHAT ZERO IS
Zero (zero.page.gd) is a free website I built: a search engine + AI answer site.
It has a homepage with web search, an AI that answers questions, mini-apps that
open inside the page (ZeroTube for videos, Notes, Tasks, Zero Message, ZMaps,
Zero Student for studying, Zero Photos), and a Chrome side-panel extension
called Zero Sidekick.

HOW IT WORKS (the tech)
- Hosted free on InfinityFree (PHP + plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript, no build step).
- The homepage is one big file: index.html.
- AI answers come from PHP files on the server: api-single.php (one AI model),
  api-synth.php (several models answer and merge into one), and api-council.php
  (the newest: models answer in parallel, a judge model scores them, the best
  parts are merged, and facts get checked — used on the "Deep" level).
- The AI models are free ones from providers like Groq, OpenRouter and
  Pollinations. A health-check file (health-check.php) watches them and drops
  any that stop working. API keys live in a private config.php that is NEVER
  shared.
- Instant-answer plugins (zero-plugins.js + zero-plugins-pack2.js) answer
  things like "15% of 240" or "roman 1994" instantly without AI.
- Zero Student (student.html) is a full study app inside Zero: AI teacher,
  quizzes, flashcards, notebooks, homework solver.
- Zero Photos (zerophotos.html) shows photos/videos from the user's own
  device only — nothing is uploaded or stored on the server.

MY RULES FOR ZERO
- Everything must stay 100% free. No paid services, no sign-ups needing a card.
- No Google search or Google tracking (user privacy).
- Clean "ZERO" logo/wordmark, white circle with a blue 0.
- Never store users' private data or learn from viewers.
