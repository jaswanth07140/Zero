Everything below is copy-paste ready for https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole

## 1. Name
Zero Computer

## 2. Short description (max 132 chars)
Let Zero AI drive this Chrome browser for you. You watch — the AI does the work.

## 3. Detailed description
**Zero Computer gives you an AI that uses your browser for you — for free.**

Type "computer: <task>" in Zero AI at zero.page.gd, and watch as the AI navigates pages,
reads content, clicks buttons, and fills in forms — step by step, with a live activity log
and screenshots, right inside Zero.

**What it can do**
- Navigate to websites and read page content
- Click links and buttons
- Fill in form fields (never password fields)
- Capture screenshots so you can watch it work

**What it will never do**
- Touch password fields or log in as you
- Buy anything or make payments
- Run without you starting the task

**How it works**
1. Install the extension — it generates its own private pairing key on your device.
2. Open Zero AI (zero.page.gd/zerocode.html), tap the gear icon, and paste your key.
3. Type "computer: ..." and watch it go.

Your pairing key never leaves your device. Each viewer gets their own key, so it works
for everyone — not just one person.

**Requirements:** A desktop computer running Chrome or Edge. Mobile Chrome cannot run
extensions.

Free forever. No account. No card.

Privacy policy: https://zero.page.gd/zero-privacy.html

4. Paste the name, descriptions, and category above
5. Set privacy policy URL: https://zero.page.gd/zero-privacy.html
6. Add your screenshots
7. Submit for review (usually approved within a few days)
