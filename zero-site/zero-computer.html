Zero Computer

Type one task in Zero AI — the AI drives your Chrome browser by itself. You just watch.
Just describe what you want (“go to Google and type YouTube”) — or start with computer:. Both work.

You need a computer with Chrome or Edge. Phones and tablets can't run browser extensions.

Set it up in 2 minutes

Download Zero Computer
1
Unzip the downloadWindows: right-click → Extract All. Mac: double-click. Chromebook: open the Files app, double-click the zip, click Extract. You'll get a folder called zero-computer-extension.
2
Load it in ChromeGo to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked, and select the zero-computer-extension folder.
3
Copy your pairing keyClick the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, pin the "0" icon, then click it. Hit Copy next to your pairing key — this secret key is yours alone.
4
Paste it in Zero AIOpen Zero AI, tap the gear icon (top right), paste the key, hit Save. Done — you never touch settings again.
5
Give it a taskType computer: find me the cheapest NYC to London flight — or just say it plainly, Zero detects computer tasks automatically. Watch it work. Keep Chrome open while it drives.

How it works

Your key → Zero relay → your extension. Zero AI posts your task to the relay at zero.page.gd; the extension picks it up instantly (with a 30-second poll as backup) and the AI plans up to 12 steps ahead, showing you a live log with screenshots. Commands expire after 2 hours and are never stored long-term.
Pages the AI can't touch: chrome://, about:, and extension pages are blocked by Chrome itself — the AI will tell you instead of failing silently.

What the AI can and can't do

It can: open pages, click buttons, fill in search boxes and forms, read results, take screenshots, and report back — all while you watch every step.
It never: touches password fields, logs in as you, or buys/pays for anything. You handle logins and payments yourself.

Troubleshooting

My files say " (1)" like content (1).js

Your browser added that because the files already existed. Right-click each one → Rename → delete the " (1)" so the names are exactly manifest.json, background.js, content.js, popup.html, popup.js. Then move them into the extension folder, replacing the old ones.

It still shows the old version after refresh

On chrome://extensions, click Remove under Zero Computer, then Load unpacked and select the folder again with the new files. (You'll get a fresh pairing key — copy the new one.)

The AI says the computer timed out

Make sure Chrome is open on your computer with the extension enabled (its toggle is blue on chrome://extensions). The computer must stay on while the AI works.

Is my key safe?

Yes. It's a random 32-character code that lives only on your computer and in your browser's settings. Nobody — not even Zero's makers — can see it or drive your browser without it.

Does it work on my phone or iPad?

No — phones and tablets can't run browser extensions, so the AI can't drive a mobile browser. Use the Zero website on your phone; set up Zero Computer on any desktop or laptop with Chrome or Edge.

Do I have to type “computer:” every time?

No. Zero now detects computer tasks automatically from plain descriptions like “go to Google and type YouTube”. The computer: prefix still works if you prefer it.
