Zero Computer — install in 2 minutes (Chrome on your computer)

WHAT IT DOES
  Lets Zero AI drive YOUR Chrome browser by itself: open pages, click,
  fill forms, read results, take screenshots. You just watch.

INSTALL
  1. On your computer, open Chrome and go to: chrome://extensions
  2. Turn ON "Developer mode" (toggle, top-right).
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and select the "zero-extension" folder
     (the one containing manifest.json).
  4. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin
     next to "Zero Computer" so it stays visible.
  5. Click the "0" icon in the toolbar -> click your pairing key to copy it.
  6. Go to https://zero.page.gd/zerocode.html -> Settings ->
     paste the key into "Computer key" -> Save.

USE
  Keep this Chrome open. In Zero AI, say e.g.:
    "computer: find the cheapest NYC to London flight on Skyscanner"
  Zero AI drives the browser itself and reports back with a screenshot.
  You don't touch anything — just watch.

NOTES
  - The AI will never touch password fields and never completes purchases.
  - Your computer must be on with Chrome open for it to work.
  - To stop it anytime: turn the extension off at chrome://extensions.
